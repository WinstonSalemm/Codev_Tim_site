## Overview

Codev_Tim Travel is a CRM for travel agencies with built-in AI. It gives agencies one workspace for managing clients and leads, organizing tours, and supporting everyday operations. Status: In Development.

AI is embedded into the CRM workflows to help teams process inquiries faster, structure information, and support managers during client work.

## Problem Statement

Travel agencies often split leads, conversations, and trip details across spreadsheets and messaging apps. Codev_Tim Travel brings those workflows into one CRM and adds AI tools directly to the agency's operational workspace.

## Business Context

The product is aimed at travel agencies that need one operating workspace for managers working with clients, leads, and tours.

## System Blueprint

CRM client → operational services → data store. AI is connected to the CRM workflows and helps process operational information.

## Technology Stack

The current product definition records CRM and AI Integration; the concrete implementation stack will be specified as development progresses.

## Trade-offs

The priority is useful manager workflows and reliable CRM data. AI features are added where they reduce manual work while keeping the user in control.

## Roadmap

The next step is to define the core travel-agency workflows and extend the CRM contour with AI support.

## Interface Record

The primary interface is a travel-agency manager workspace for clients, leads, tours, and AI assistance.

## Lessons Recorded

For a travel CRM, AI should be part of the operational workflow rather than a separate chat beside it.
