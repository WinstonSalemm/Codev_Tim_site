## Overview

Call Tracker is an internal tool for tracking calls and customer communication.

## Problem Statement

Contact history and commitments need to remain available after each conversation.

## Business Context

Staff log communication outcomes while managers review team activity.

## System Blueprint

The web interface uses a Next.js server, with Prisma accessing PostgreSQL.

## Technology Stack

Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Prisma and PostgreSQL.

## Trade-offs

Staff record conversation outcomes. The usefulness of the history depends on complete entries.

## Roadmap

Further changes are driven by everyday customer communication needs.

## Interface Record

Customer records, call history, planned follow-ups and reports.

## Lessons Recorded

Useful communication tracking records both the outcome and the next action.
